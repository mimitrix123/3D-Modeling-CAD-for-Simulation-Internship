# Assembly Plan

## Bill of materials

1. Tray floor
2. Tray front/back/left/right walls
3. Phone base, back support, and lip
4. Pen-cup shell
5. Snap-fit divider and finger tab
6. Cable-management insert and grommet
7. Four rubber feet

## Assembly sequence

1. Install rubber feet on the underside of the tray.
2. Fit the tray walls around the floor.
3. Slide the phone-support pieces into their locating region.
4. Insert the cable-management component at the front cable path.
5. Slide the divider into the organizer channel.
6. Place the pen cup into the right-side storage bay.

## Exploded-view intent

The Blender animation moves removable modules away from the tray between frames 45 and 90, then returns them to the assembled configuration by frame 130. This communicates assembly order and part relationships for a portfolio presentation.

## Design-for-assembly notes

- Use captive/locating geometry where possible.
- Keep removable inserts tool-free.
- Avoid unnecessarily tight FDM fits; tune clearance using actual printer/material calibration.
- Round external edges to improve handling and reduce stress concentration.
