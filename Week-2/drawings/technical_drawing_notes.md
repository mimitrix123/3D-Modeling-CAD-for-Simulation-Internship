# Week 2 Technical Drawing Notes

- Units: mm
- Gear module: 2
- Gear thickness: 12
- Shaft diameter: Ø12
- Base plate: 220 × 100 × 10
- Gear 1: 20T, pitch Ø40, nominal outside Ø44
- Gear 2: 30T, pitch Ø60, nominal outside Ø64
- Gear 3: 40T, pitch Ø80, nominal outside Ø84
- G1–G2 center distance: 50
- G2–G3 center distance: 70

Two external gear meshes reverse rotation twice, so input and output rotate in the same direction. The 20T-to-40T pair gives a 2:1 reduction.
